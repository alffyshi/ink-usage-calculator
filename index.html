FAQ: Estimating Printing Ink Usage

How the calculator works, what is included, and how to get from estimate to exact quote.

How is theoretical ink consumption calculated?
Ink demand is driven by three variables: the printed area, the wet ink laydown (set by your anilox roll in flexo, or the cylinder depth in gravure), and the ink's specific gravity. Our calculator starts from the wet-film volume of your chosen anilox or plate, applies substrate and press-speed corrections, and returns the total kilogram estimate — the classic Area × Laydown × Density relationship, fully automated.
Does this calculator account for printing waste?
Yes — a dynamic waste factor is built into every result, so you don't need to add a margin yourself. Setup and make-ready cost proportionally more on short runs, so the calculator applies 18% for jobs under 2,000 m², 12% for 2,000–10,000 m² and 8% above 10,000 m². Press speeds above 200 m/min add a small extra allowance for misting and volatility.
Why is accurate ink estimation important for my budget?
Over-estimating ties up capital in ink you may never print — and water-based inks have a shelf life. Under-estimating risks a mid-run shortage and a production stop that costs far more than the ink. A reliable estimate also lets you quote packaging jobs competitively instead of guessing.
What factors affect ink usage in Flexo and Gravure?
The biggest lever is your ink delivery hardware: anilox roll volume (BCM) in flexo and cylinder depth in gravure. Then comes substrate absorbency (paper pulls more ink than film), print coverage, press speed and the pigment concentration of the series you choose. Our substrate compatibility guide explains how each surface behaves.
Can I use this for custom color matching?
Yes. The volume calculation is the same; custom Pantone matches may have a slightly different density, which our lab confirms when quoting. If you're planning a custom color, start with our color matching service — every match is verified with a drawdown proof and held to a ΔE ≤ 2 batch tolerance before you order.
How accurate is the estimate — and how do I get an exact number?
For standard 4-color (CMYK) process work, results typically land within ±10–15% of real consumption. Heavy white on film, dense spot colors or highly absorbent kraft can shift the number. For an exact figure, send us your artwork and substrate: we run a lab drawdown and confirm the ink demand per color — free 5 kg samples ship within 72 hours, and production orders under 1 ton dispatch within 72 hours as well.
